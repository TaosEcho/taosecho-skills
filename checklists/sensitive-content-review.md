# Sensitive Content Review

Use before committing examples, reports, screenshots, logs, or public docs.

## Blocked Content

- Real user private data.
- Internal operating addresses, credentials, tokens, cookies, or local auth files.
- Real product identifiers in examples.
- Real brand names in examples unless the file is explicitly public-source commentary.
- Supplier, cost, margin, return, ad, or support records from a live business.
- Host-specific secrets or local machine configuration.

## Placeholder Rules

- Product IDs use `SAMPLE_ASIN_*`.
- Product names use `Sample Product *`.
- Brands use `Sample Brand *`.
- User-provided business data uses generic examples.
- Public-source examples cite the source and separate sample data from real data.
